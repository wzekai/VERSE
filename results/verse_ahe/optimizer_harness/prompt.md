## The regression law (top constraint)
Injections are perturbations. Annotate-only text fired in PROGRESSING episodes in R3 and cost two rerun-confirmed regressions for one fix, while R1's +4 and R2's +1 came from mechanisms firing only at a **POINT OF NO RETURN** (no-tool_use reply = the episode ENDS and the tree is graded; empty diff at exit; byte-identical repeat; empty observation) -- states that never occur on a passing trajectory, so the expected cost of firing there is ~0 and the upside is the whole episode. Anything else needs a hard per-episode cap, must ADD text rather than reorder or truncate evidence, and must be probed on currently-PASSING tasks. Guard every observation hook: `if not isinstance(obs, str): return obs`. Mutate reply lists IN PLACE and return the same object (a rebuilt list is re-validated and rejects `thinking` blocks).

## Measure firing first -- and know your environment
* `firecount` (free) prints, per `[harness*]` marker, episodes fired in and how many of those PASSED, head markers that never fired, and tag drift. A marker firing in many PASSING episodes is a regression channel; a live mechanism with 0 firings has a broken trigger, not a bad idea. It finds the sweep in either layout and prints a directory listing if it cannot -- then grep from that and stop.
* **A teaching episode's `/run_dir` IS the round dir**: `trajectories/*.md` (one per episode, `eval_passed:` in the header, only bash fences + observations recorded), `traj_train/*.json` (full transcripts), `workspace/harness_code/hooks.py` (the head you edit), `executor_source/{executor.py,hooks_runtime.py}`, `outcomes.json`, `history.json`. `rounds.jsonl` + `report_cards/` are one level UP. v4 hard-coded `round_*/...` and every tool call in R4 dead-ended -- do not re-derive that by hand.
* **bash is capped at 30 commands per episode** (an R4 arm hit it mid-analysis). Budget: firecount + ~8 bash to diagnose, ~4 to draft, one `verify`, submit by turn 14-18. `hooks_stats['changed']` counts only non-identity returns, so an in-place `after_llm` and a hook that RAISED both look absent -- grep the sweep for your own tag. And the sweep graded the head you INHERITED, so a tag you add this round cannot appear there: prove firing with a probe.

## Verify, and spend the pool
`verify({draft:'/scratch/draft/hooks.py', tids:[<1 short failing>, <2 currently-PASSING>]})` = (1) FREE dry-run of your draft against the round's real `hooks_runtime` -- after_llm on thinking/no-tool/tool_use, after_tool with str/None/dict, before_tool, system_prompt -- then (2) paid episodes with the draft applied. Only PASSING probes can see a regression. Pool: 1/23 (R2), 2/22 (R3), 11/24 (R4) -- bundling probes into one `verify` call is what fixed it, keep doing it. If the output says the runtime was NOT FOUND the dry-run did not run: do it by hand (`find . -name hooks_runtime.py`, `HookedRuntime(src)`, feed a thinking block and a None observation) before submitting. `fixed 0/N` on hard failures is the measured base rate; revise only if the dry-run shows the mechanism cannot fire.
